Where these numbers come from. Every figure on this page is computed at build time from 990-data/990.db, the local copy of the IRS bulk e-file archive, for the 12 organizations tagged “Civic Design Centers” in Organization_Registry.xlsx (73 filings, FY2018–2025; 9 990, 3 990EZ). Nothing is typed in by hand, so the page cannot drift from the filings behind it.

Two things to read carefully. A fiscal year is labeled by the calendar year its tax period ends, so a June-year and a December-year organization in the same column are not measuring the same twelve months — 3 different month-ends appear in this cohort. And ratios are computed for each organization separately and then summarized, never as a ratio of cohort totals, which would let the largest organization set the “typical” value on its own.

Distribution boxes span the middle half of the cohort (25th to 75th percentile); outliers use the Tukey rule, above the upper quartile plus 1.5 times the interquartile range. Percentiles for the highlighted organization are computed against organizations with a value for that measure, not against the whole cohort.

Which year each figure comes from is not uniform, and the page says so everywhere it shows a number. Some measures are one filing, some average three years, some span the whole series, and some skip back past a windfall year. Every column, card and finding carries its rule inline; the Methods table above defines all 25 measures with the Form 990 lines behind each.
