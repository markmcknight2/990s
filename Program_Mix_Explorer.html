Methods

Figures are averages of each organization's latest three 990 filings in the local archive (990-data/990.db). Fiscal years are labelled by the year the tax period ends, so a September-year and a December-year organization in the same column are not measuring the same twelve months. Every stream is a share of the organization's own earned revenue: programs, camps, tuition, admissions, memberships, rentals, store and plants, which keeps capital gifts out of the comparison. Membership dues (line 1b) are counted as earned revenue. The budget and contributed figures in the context table are for scale only. Revenue lines are classified by the first matching rule below, then by inputs/program_line_overrides.csv. Part III activities are classified by their heading first, with these hand calls:

  • 581275604:4d → education: FY2023 describes this activity as education programs and advice to the public; later years reword it as 'operates a wildlife clinic to provide education programs', but the clinic itself is reported separately as 4c.
